You do not need to provide your NHS number, but it can help us match your details correctly.

This is a 10 digit number, like 485 777 3456.
Where to find your NHS number
You can find your NHS number:
  • by signing into the NHS App or your account on the NHS website (opens in a new tab)
  • on any document or letter sent to you by the NHS, such as a prescription, test result or appointment letter
  • by contacting your GP surgery
